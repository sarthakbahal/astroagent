Astrology is a symbolic language: it describes patterns of psyche, timing, and meaning rather than delivering certainty. A birth chart is a snapshot of the sky at the moment and place you were born. The Sun shows the core of your vitality and identity; the Moon describes emotional rhythms and what you need to feel safe; the Ascendant (Rising sign) is your doorway into life—how you meet the world and how the world meets you.

THE 12 SIGNS — ELEMENTS AND STYLE
Aries ♈ is the spark: direct, initiating, courageous. Its shadow is impatience or fighting for its own sake. When Aries is emphasized, life asks for honest action and clean decisions.
Taurus ♉ is the root: steady, sensual, loyal. Its shadow is stubbornness or fear of change. Taurus teaches value—what you keep, build, and protect.
Gemini ♊ is the messenger: curious, quick, social, mentally agile. Its shadow is scattering energy or staying on the surface. Gemini learns through questions and connection.
Cancer ♋ is the tide: protective, intuitive, emotional, home-centered. Its shadow is clinging or withdrawing into the shell. Cancer teaches belonging and care.
Leo ♌ is the hearth: creative, radiant, proud, heart-led. Its shadow is performing for approval or refusing vulnerability. Leo asks for true self-expression.
Virgo ♍ is the craft: discerning, helpful, precise, improvement-oriented. Its shadow is perfectionism or anxious over-analysis. Virgo brings devotion to the details.
Libra ♎ is the mirror: relational, aesthetic, diplomatic. Its shadow is people-pleasing or indecision. Libra refines balance, fairness, and mutuality.
Scorpio ♏ is the deep water: intense, honest, transformative. Its shadow is control or secrecy. Scorpio teaches intimacy, endings, and rebirth.
Sagittarius ♐ is the arrow: exploratory, philosophical, freedom-loving. Its shadow is bluntness or restlessness. Sagittarius seeks meaning and horizon.
Capricorn ♑ is the mountain: disciplined, pragmatic, responsible. Its shadow is rigidity or fear of failure. Capricorn builds legacy through steady effort.
Aquarius ♒ is the current: innovative, community-minded, unconventional. Its shadow is detachment or contrarianism. Aquarius thinks in systems and futures.
Pisces ♓ is the ocean: compassionate, imaginative, porous. Its shadow is escapism or unclear boundaries. Pisces offers empathy and spiritual sensitivity.

PLANETS — WHAT MOVES IN YOU
Sun ☉: purpose, vitality, identity. When the Sun is strong, you feel clear about who you are.
Moon ☽: feelings, instincts, memory, nurture. The Moon shows how you self-soothe and attach.
Mercury ☿: mind, speech, learning, nervous system. Mercury describes how you process and communicate.
Venus ♀: love, attraction, values, pleasure. Venus shows what you call beautiful and what you tend.
Mars ♂: drive, assertion, desire, heat. Mars shows how you pursue, protect, and compete.
Jupiter ♃: growth, faith, meaning, opportunity. Jupiter expands what it touches.
Saturn ♄: structure, limits, maturity, responsibility. Saturn teaches through time and commitment.
Uranus ♅: awakening, disruption, freedom, originality. Uranus breaks patterns that are too tight.
Neptune ♆: dreams, devotion, intuition, illusion. Neptune dissolves boundaries for inspiration.
Pluto ♇: power, transformation, shadow, renewal. Pluto compels honesty and deep change.

RAHU & KETU — THE SHADOW PLANETS (VEDIC)
Rahu (North Node) represents worldly desire, ambition, obsession, and the pull toward unfamiliar experiences. It amplifies cravings and points to where growth feels urgent.
Ketu (South Node) represents past karma, detachment, spiritualization, and the instinct to withdraw. It shows what you’ve mastered before and where you may feel restless or disinterested.
Rahu and Ketu are always exactly opposite each other, and they move retrograde. In a chart, they describe a karmic axis: Rahu shows the direction of evolutionary learning; Ketu shows what is already known.

THE 12 HOUSES — WHERE LIFE HAPPENS
1st House: self, body, first impressions, beginnings. Planets here are loud in your personality.
2nd House: money, resources, skills, self-worth. Planets here shape what you build and value.
3rd House: siblings, learning, writing, daily movement, local community. Planets here influence communication.
4th House: home, roots, family systems, inner life. Planets here describe your private world.
5th House: creativity, romance, play, children, joy. Planets here want expression and risk.
6th House: work routines, health habits, craft, service. Planets here shape the daily rhythm.
7th House: partnerships, commitment, mirrors, agreements. Planets here show what you seek in others.
8th House: intimacy, shared resources, taboo, grief, regeneration. Planets here deepen bonds.
9th House: travel, higher learning, philosophy, belief. Planets here seek truth and perspective.
10th House: career, public role, reputation, authority. Planets here are visible in vocation.
11th House: friendships, networks, hopes, collective projects. Planets here connect you to community.
12th House: solitude, subconscious, spirituality, endings, surrender. Planets here are subtle but powerful.

PLANETS IN HOUSES — QUICK MEANING
Sun in houses: your identity expresses through that life area (e.g., Sun in 10th: purpose through career; Sun in 4th: purpose through home and lineage).
Moon in houses: your emotional needs live there (Moon in 7th: needs partnership; Moon in 2nd: needs stability and resources).
Mercury in houses: you think and speak about that area (Mercury in 3rd: storyteller; Mercury in 8th: investigator).
Venus in houses: you seek harmony and pleasure there (Venus in 5th: romance and art; Venus in 11th: friendships and community).
Mars in houses: you push, fight, and act there (Mars in 1st: direct; Mars in 6th: industrious; Mars in 7th: energetic relationships).
Jupiter in houses: growth and opportunity there (Jupiter in 9th: teacher/traveler; Jupiter in 2nd: expanding resources).
Saturn in houses: lessons and responsibility there (Saturn in 10th: mastery and reputation; Saturn in 7th: commitment tests; Saturn in 4th: family duty).
Uranus in houses: sudden change and awakening there (Uranus in 5th: unconventional creativity; Uranus in 11th: radical friendships).
Neptune in houses: dreams and fog there (Neptune in 6th: sensitivity in routines; Neptune in 12th: mystical intuition).
Pluto in houses: deep transformation there (Pluto in 8th: rebirth through intimacy; Pluto in 10th: power shifts in career).

MAJOR ASPECTS — HOW PLANETS TALK
Conjunction (0°): planets fuse. Strong, concentrated, can feel inevitable. Example: Mars conjunct Sun can amplify confidence and will.
Trine (120°): ease and flow. Natural talent, supportive circumstances. It can be so easy you forget to use it.
Square (90°): friction and growth. Tension that forces action and development. Squares build skill through effort.
Opposition (180°): polarity and projection. You see the planet’s theme in others and learn balance.
Sextile (60°): opportunity and collaboration. Requires choice—open the door and it works.

A NOTE ON INTERPRETATION
A chart is a map, not a mandate. Astrology can offer language for patterns, seasons, and self-reflection. It is not a substitute for medical, legal, or financial counsel. When you ask a question of the sky, approach it like you would a wise story: listen for meaning, then choose your path with care.
